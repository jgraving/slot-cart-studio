How Cart Studio Works

Four steps

  1. Choose your SD card. Pick the top of the card, the folder with Games in it.
  2. Cart Studio will look up art for your games.
  3. Tweak any cart. Click it to swap the label for a logo or your own art, or change the shell colour.
  4. Save. Chrome and Edge write straight to the card. Safari and Firefox give you a ZIP and walk you through unpacking it to your card.

Making your own labels

Save a PNG named after the game in that system’s folder: Labels/GBA/Metroid Fusion.png goes with Games/GBA/Metroid Fusion.gba.
